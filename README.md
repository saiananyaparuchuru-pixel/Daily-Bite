DailyBite is an AI-powered meal planning and food recommendation platform designed especially for students living in PGs, hostels, and shared apartments who struggle to find affordable, healthy, and convenient daily meals.

The platform allows students to explore available meals, set their food preferences, specify their daily food budget, and create personalized meal plans for 3 or 7 days. Based on their preferences, budget, and food choices, DailyBite recommends suitable meals.
Key Features
1. Personalized Meal Recommendations: Recommends meals based on dietary preferences, food type, category, and budget.
2. Smart Meal Planner: Allows students to plan their meals for 3 or 7 days.
3. Budget Management: Helps students select meals according to their affordable daily budget.
4. Food Preferences: Supports vegetarian and non-vegetarian preferences.
5. AI Voice Assistant: Integrates Gnani AI APIs to make the platform voice-enabled.
Gnani AI Integration
1. Speech-to-Text (STT) – Gnani Prisma
Integrated Gnani AI's Speech-to-Text API to convert users' spoken instructions into text.
 Users can give voice commands instead of manually applying filters.
For example:
- "Show me vegetarian meals."
- "Find meals under 100 rupees."
- "Show me non-vegetarian food."
2. Text-to-Speech (TTS) – Gnani Timbre
Integrated Gnani AI's Text-to-Speech API to convert text responses into audio, allowing the platform to communicate recommendations and responses through voice.
For example, when a student asks for vegetarian meals under ₹100, the platform can respond through audio with suitable meal recommendations.
